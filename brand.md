# DoubleTake visual direction

An instrument maker's steampunk observatory: antique brass hardware, charcoal enamel, engraved typography, parchment price readouts, and a bespoke twin-dial comparator.

An editorial introduction leads into a comfortable research workstation. The hero machine is decorative; live figures appear only in labeled data panels. Observed pool gaps and router returns stay visibly separate.

Colors are centralized in src/style.css. Brass #d1ad72 is the accent; charcoal #121a19 is the page, #192321 the raised surface; ivory #eee4cf is primary text; parchment #e7ddc5 contains dark #302c22 readouts. Muted red communicates negative quoted returns. Positive quotes remain amber because they are unverified.

Cormorant Garamond provides the engraved editorial voice. IBM Plex Mono provides tabular numerical precision. System sans-serif keeps descriptions readable. Fonts are self-hosted and licensed under OFL.

Motion is a finite mechanical startup in the decorative hero and brief control feedback. Respect reduced motion. Never animate fictional price history, use ornamental gauges as data, or allow material styling to obscure a status or action.
